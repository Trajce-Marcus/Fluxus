# Inspections — domestic building inspections (Victoria)

Conducting inspections of homes in metropolitan Melbourne and producing the
inspection reports. Quotes are recorded as a price only; billing is out of
scope. A test of how Fluxus goes, not a move into the inspection-software
market.

Three job types: Pre-Purchase Inspection, Mandatory Stage Inspection and
Independent Stage Inspection. Pre-Purchase comes first.

- [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md) — the design (Pre-Purchase only)
- [docs/sample-data/](docs/sample-data/) — inspection areas, defects, code
  references and the mappings between them
